Commonly asked.

What should I expect on my first visit?

Your first visit begins with a cleanse, followed by a skin reading — a conversation to understand your skin’s history, habits, and what it needs right now. From there, we build a treatment around you.

What’s included in a facial?

No two facials at Mei Skin look the same. Each is built around your skin, in the moment — drawing from deep-cleansing bubble packs for effortless extractions, ultrasonic and manual extraction techniques, a range of AHA/BHA/PHA peels suited to your skin’s needs, and LED and micro-current for repair and lift. We layer in sculpting and pressure-point massage to release tension and support lymphatic drainage, RF lifting around the eyes and face, and high-frequency plasma treatment to stimulate collagen — finished with layered, modeling masks and products chosen to work in tandem with each device, not just alongside it. A 60-minute visit (The Mei Glow) gives us time to cover the essentials with intention. A 90-minute visit (The Mei Reset) opens up space to move deeper — addressing more than one concern in a single session, without ever feeling rushed.

How long are appointments, and how often should I come?

Sessions currently run either 60 to 90 minutes, depending on how much time you want to give your skin that day. We recommend a treatment every 4–6 weeks to achieve and maintain results.

Do I need to do anything to prepare for my visit?

We recommend arriving without makeup if you can — every visit starts with a double cleanse, so it will come off either way, but skipping it beforehand means we can start reading your skin right away. And if you’re wearing sunscreen when you walk in, even better — we love that. In the week leading up to your visit, please avoid retinol and exfoliating products, and please book at least a month after a chemical peel, microneedling, or facial laser treatment.

What should I do after my visit?

You’re welcome to cleanse as usual at the end of the day. The one habit we’ll always encourage: wear sunscreen every day, rain or shine. Even on cloudy days, UV rays still reach your skin, so keeping that protection consistent is one of the best things you can do for your results.

Do you sell products?

Absolutely. We have retail products from Dermabell, Éclat du Teint, Dr. esthé, and more available to purchase on-site. We would be delighted to recommend products for your home care routine should you be interested.

Where do I park?

There’s paid underground parking directly below the studio at 2080 West Broadway. Free street parking is also available along Maple St, Cypress St, and W 10th, 11th, and 12th.

What’s your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 48 hours’ notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a 50% fee, and cancellations within 24 hours are subject to a 100% fee.
